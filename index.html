Morph Age

Morph Age is the definitive Mac application for morphing faces and warping images. Define precise curves on your images to create seamless distortions and transformations, with real-time previews powered by advanced GPU rendering. Updated for macOS 26 Tahoe, it delivers sub-pixel accuracy, layers, and colorizing for professional results.

100% Native Mac Experience

Built exclusively for macOS, Morph Age imports from iPhoto, iSight, or any source. Export QuickTime animations directly to Final Cut Pro, iMovie, or iOS devices. Modernized with Liquid Glass icons, SF Symbols, and toolbar enhancements for seamless Tahoe integration.

Precise Curve Control

Animate warp curves independently—eyes first, then mouth—with springy motion effects. Non-linear blending mixes images at any percentage. Zoom for sub-pixel accuracy, eliminating ghosting, all visible in real-time interactive previews.

Advanced Effects

Colorize curves for Hulk-like warps, with animated bleeding over deformations. Use layers to isolate morphs on specific areas against static backgrounds. Real-time GPU rendering handles large images effortlessly, even mid-animation adjustments.
